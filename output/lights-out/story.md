# Lights Out

**Rule:** It can only move in the dark.  
**Logline:** When a blackout hits the city, a girl who has been scared of the dark her whole life discovers the giant in the street can only move when the lights are off — and that its own shadow counts.  
**Genre:** monster thriller · **Set:** city_block · **Look:** night_blue · **Runtime:** 65.0s · **Shots:** 32

## 2 · Characters

- **Mia** (girl voice) — 14. Has slept with a lamp on every night of her life and carries a flashlight everywhere. Want: get home. Flaw: afraid of the dark — which turns out to be expertise.
- **Officer Reyes** (cop voice) — Runs the barricade. Trusts procedure until procedure runs out of fuel.

## 3 · The monster — The Dimwalker

A 56 m stone colossus with ice-blue fissures. Statue-still in any light; the instant a patch of it is in darkness, that part can move.  
Build: procedural `colossus`, 56 m tall, glow RGB [0.3, 0.85, 1.0], 3 eyes. Signature: one huge step per heartbeat of darkness; a low grinding roar; ground-cracking stomps.

## 4 · Outline

1. **Hook** (0-4s) — Blackout ripples across the skyline; a giant takes a step in the dark.
2. **Rule** (4-11s) — Floodlights snap on; it freezes mid-stride. It only moves in the dark.
3. **Hero** (11-18s) — Mia, scared of the dark her whole life, flashlight in hand.
4. **Escalation 1** (18-28s) — Generator dying; every flicker, it is closer (jump-scare cuts).
5. **Reversal** (28-40s) — Its shadow: the hand behind its back is in darkness — it rips a floodlight down.
6. **Clever use** (40-50s) — Mia: light it from everywhere. Cars ring it, beams up. No shadows.
7. **All is lost** (50-60s) — Generator dies. Total dark. It lunges at Mia.
8. **Win** (60-70s) — Mia does what she does every night: clicks on her flashlight — into its eye. Frozen.
9. **Ending** (70-85s) — Lights blaze. It never moves again; a park is built around it. Mia sleeps with the lights off.

## 6 · Critique (ruthless)

- Hook is 19 words — far too slow for a 2-second hook. Cut to the time stamp + the step.
- 'Scientists would later learn' breaks tension and is a flash-forward nobody needs. Show the rule, then say it in 7 words.
- Mia's flaw is told, not shown — give her the flashlight in the first shot she appears in, so the payoff is planted.
- The flicker escalation needs a visual rule the audience can track: each flicker = a jump cut, monster visibly closer.
- The reversal (shadow) is great but explained flat — split into 'saw its shadow' / 'its hand was moving' for a beat of dread.
- The ending tells us she's 'not afraid anymore' — too neat. Better button: she sleeps with the lights OFF (image, not statement).
- No silence anywhere. Add a full music drop when the generator dies; let the dark sit for a second.
- Win is a cheat if the flashlight wasn't established — it is now (planted in the hero beat, used in the climax).
- Too many 'then's. Dialogue should be shouted fragments, not sentences.
