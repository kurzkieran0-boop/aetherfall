# The Kindness Meter

**Rule:** A number above everyone's head counts how many times they've helped someone.  
**Logline:** In a city where a glowing number over every head counts your good deeds, the only person stuck at a red zero is the one who never helps for an audience — and when meteors fall, he's the only one who stays.  
**Genre:** emotional drama / disaster · **Set:** meter_city · **Look:** kindness · **Runtime:** 154.9s · **Shots:** 73

- TELL vs SHOW — v1 narrates every event. FIX: narrator only bridges; the rule is SHOWN (door slam, kid backing away, a +1 that only ticks when phones rise).
- TWIST FAIRNESS — v1's infinity comes from nowhere ('true hero all along'). - EMOTION — v1 ends on a moral. FIX: silence before the flip; nobody speaks at the payoff; the shopkeeper who slammed the door now holds it; a quotable last line that reframes the title ('the only kind that counts').
