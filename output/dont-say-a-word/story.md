## 6 · Critique (ruthless)

- Opening sentence is 18 words — the hook must be two short punches: 'It has no eyes.' / 'It doesn't need them.'
- 'Instead it hunts by listening' is soft. The rule needs to be 5 words and then PROVEN by a kill-shot cut mid-sentence.
- Show the cop getting cut off mid-word — the cut IS the rule. Don't narrate it afterwards.
- Theo's flaw is told. Plant it with a specific (report cards) and make his silence physical (hand over Ava's mouth, no line).
- Phone beat is good but needs thunder + siren too: 'Only voices' is the rule clarification and a mini-reveal.
- Radio plan must read in one line: 'He talked. Just not from where he was standing.'
- Reversal is cheap if it's random — the rain is set up from shot 1, so the rain killing the radio is earned.
- 'I think I'll stay quiet' is a statement; 'I think I'm good' is funnier and more natural. Narrator button lands the irony.
- Need a silent beat: when the street collapses, drop the score completely.
