# The Dry Side

**Rule:** It can't cross water.  
**Logline:** A giant made of fire can't cross the canal — and Nora's little brother is stuck on its side. When it starts building a bridge out of the city itself, Nora floods the plaza and goes back for him.

**Picked #1:** The canal is a visible, physical line the audience can track in every wide shot; the rule creates instant geography (safe side / dry side). The giant beating its own rule by building a bridge is a reversal people won't see coming, and the hero's flaw (always goes back) becomes the climax.

## 6 · Critique (ruthless)

- Hook buries the rule; open on the image (everyone running) and the reason in the second line — curiosity gap.
- 'Cannot cross water' reads like a manual; show the foot hitting the water first, then say it in five words.
- Leo being left behind needs its own beat and a cut to him alone and tiny under the giant — that's the stakes shot.
- Nora's flaw has no setup. Add 'Nora never left anything behind' BEFORE she needs to go back, so the climax pays it off.
- The bridge reversal is the best idea in the story — slow it down: 'It can't cross water… but it can fill it.'
- Flood plan needs a rule-echo line: 'If it can't cross water — give it nothing but water.'
- Leo trapped on the island is a second twist; land it with a braam, not narration alone.
- 'Of course I did' is flat. 'I always come back' turns the flaw into the theme in four words.
- Missing silence/air: let the jump into the water play with just a splash and steam before the next line.
